ABOUT RAIN-TO-ROAD

Rain-to-Road is a machine learning platform that predicts real-time road travel times based on live weather, traffic congestion, and infrastructure conditions across Addis Ababa sub-cities and sefers.

THE PROBLEM

Severe rainfall and peak-hour traffic cause unpredictable delays across Addis Ababa, confusing everyday navigation tools.

OUR SOLUTION

Multiple Linear Regression models train continuously on weather and distance data to calculate accurate travel arrival estimates.

FEATURES ANALYZED

Rainfall (mm), Traffic Density (%), Temperature (°C), Departure Time, Road Classification, and Route Distance.

WHY REGRESSION?

Since travel duration is continuous, multiple linear regression provides transparent, lightweight, and precise mathematical scoring.
